Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  BEING RESIDENCES, Ahangama, Sri Lanka

This Privacy Policy explains how BEING RESIDENCES collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us when you visit our website or submit an enquiry. We are committed to handling your data with care and transparency.

1. Who We Are

BEING RESIDENCES is a boutique condominium development located in Ahangama on Sri Lanka's southern coast. References to "we", "us" or "our" in this policy refer to BEING RESIDENCES and its development team.

For any privacy-related matters, you can contact us at: hello@beingresidences.com

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect the following:

  • Your name (first and last) and title
  • Your country of residence
  • Your email address
  • Your contact phone number and country code
  • Your residence of interest (optional)
  • Your preferred method of contact (email or WhatsApp)
  • The content of your enquiry message

When you register your interest via the email sign-up form, we collect your email address.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our site may collect certain technical information automatically when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, the pages you view and the time and date of your visit. This information is used for the purposes of site security and improving the website experience. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

Our website uses the following third-party services, which may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts: to load web fonts. Google may log the request including your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap: to display a map of the development location. OpenStreetMap may log your IP address when the map tile is loaded.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about BEING RESIDENCES residences, ownership or the development
  • Send you information about the project that you have requested or consented to receive
  • Contact you via your preferred method (email or WhatsApp) as indicated on the form
  • Manage our relationship with you as a prospective purchaser
  • Comply with any legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: by submitting the enquiry form and ticking the privacy checkbox, you give us your consent to contact you in connection with your enquiry. You may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing us.
  • Legitimate interests: we have a legitimate interest in communicating with people who have expressed genuine interest in the development, provided this does not override your rights and freedoms.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, this policy is intended to comply with the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR respectively.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party.

We may share your information with:

  • Our development team and sales advisors: who need access to respond to your enquiry
  • Service providers: such as email or CRM platforms that help us manage communications, under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal or regulatory authorities: where required by law or to protect our rights

Any third parties who process data on our behalf are required to handle it securely and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and maintain a relationship with you as a prospective purchaser, typically for the duration of the development and sales period. If you request that we delete your data or withdraw your consent, we will do so promptly, subject to any legal retention obligations.

7. International Data Transfers

BEING RESIDENCES is based in Sri Lanka. If you are located in the EEA or UK, please be aware that your personal information will be transferred to and processed in Sri Lanka, which may not have data protection laws equivalent to those in your country. By submitting an enquiry, you acknowledge this transfer. We take appropriate steps to ensure your data remains protected.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access: to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: to request that inaccurate data be corrected
  • Right to erasure: to request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing: to ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability: to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@beingresidences.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may use essential session-related browser storage to support the functionality of the enquiry form. No personal data is stored in cookies.

11. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external sites such as Google Maps. Once you leave our website, we are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their own privacy policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@beingresidences.com
  • Location: BEING RESIDENCES, Ahangama, Southern Coast, Sri Lanka

If you are located in the UK or EEA and you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
