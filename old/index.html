A boutique lifestyle residence where private ownership meets effortless tropical living.

BEING RESIDENCES sits at the intersection of refined coastal living and thoughtful property ownership. Not quite a hotel, and certainly not a standard residential block, it is a professionally managed lifestyle residence where owners enjoy the benefits of a tropical retreat alongside the security and simplicity of private ownership.

Considered Design

Contemporary tropical architecture with a strong indoor-outdoor design language. Each residence blurs the line between inside and out, with open plans, generous terraces, and materials that belong in this landscape.

Professional Management

The complex is maintained to boutique hotel standards by a dedicated hospitality management team. Arrive and the residence is ready. Leave and it remains in perfect order: ownership without the operational weight.

Optional Rental Income

Owners who wish to generate income may integrate their unit into the managed rental programme. From guest reception to housekeeping, everything is handled end-to-end. Participation is entirely at the owner's discretion.
